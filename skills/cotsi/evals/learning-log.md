# Cotsi learning log

The only test that really matters is whether Cotsi makes *you* learn faster and remember longer.
This log turns that into numbers. It lives in `evals/`, so it is never packaged into the skill.

## How to use it

1. Learn a topic with Cotsi. Note roughly how many minutes it took.
2. **Next day:** in a new chat, type `/cotsi quiz me on [topic]. 5 application questions, no hints`.
   Answer from memory and record your score out of 5.
3. **Day 7:** do the same again and record that score.
4. After about 10 topics, look at the averages. Retention that holds up at day 7 is the goal; a
   day-1 score that collapses by day 7 means the reviews aren't happening, or the lessons are too
   passive.

For a before/after comparison, retest a few topics you learned with v5.2 in the same way, and
compare their day-7 scores with v6.0 topics of similar difficulty.
